Spider Design uses artificial intelligence tools to support selected internal workflows. This policy explains how we use AI responsibly and how we protect client and user interests.

## 1. Our commitment

We are committed to using AI in a way that is lawful, ethical, transparent, and aligned with professional standards.

AI tools are used to support human work, not replace human accountability.

## 2. Where AI may be used

AI may be used for tasks such as:

- Drafting and refining non-final content.
- Assisting with ideation, outlines, and early-stage research.
- Supporting coding productivity and documentation drafts.
- Internal quality support for formatting and consistency checks.

## 3. Human oversight and accountability

All material outputs delivered to clients are reviewed by a human team member before use or publication.

We do not rely on AI-generated output as final without human judgment, verification, and editing.

Responsibility for final decisions remains with Spider Design personnel.

## 4. Accuracy and verification

AI tools can produce errors, outdated information, or misleading content. We treat AI output as a draft input that must be validated.

Where factual accuracy is important, we apply independent checks before publication or delivery.

## 5. Fairness and bias

AI systems can reflect or amplify bias present in data or model behaviour.

We seek to reduce this risk by:

- Applying human review to identify problematic assumptions or language.
- Avoiding discriminatory or exclusionary use cases.
- Revising outputs that may create unfair outcomes.

## 6. Privacy and confidentiality

We do not intentionally input confidential or personally identifiable client data into third-party AI tools unless there is a clear legal basis and permission where required.

When AI-assisted workflows are used, we aim to minimise data exposure and use the least sensitive data necessary.

## 7. Intellectual property and originality

AI assistance is used to support original work, not to copy third-party protected content.

We review deliverables for originality, appropriateness, and alignment with client requirements.

## 8. Transparency

We aim to be open about our use of AI where it materially affects process or deliverables.

Clients can request information about whether AI-assisted methods were used in a project workflow.

## 9. Security and tool selection

We choose AI tools based on practicality, reliability, and available security/privacy controls.

Tool use is periodically reviewed, and we may change providers or usage patterns where risk or quality concerns arise.

## 10. Continuous improvement

AI technologies evolve quickly. We review this policy and our practices regularly to reflect legal, technical, and ethical developments.

## 11. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Spider Design through the contact form on this website and mark your message "AI Ethics Policy Enquiry".
