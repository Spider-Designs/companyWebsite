Your brand is more than a logo — it's the visual language your business uses to communicate who you are, what you do, and why customers should choose you. Done well, it builds trust before a single word is read.

Spider Design creates clear, professional brand identities for businesses at every stage — from brand new startups to established businesses looking for a refresh.

## What we create

### Logo design
A professional, versatile logo that works across your website, printed materials, social media profiles, and everywhere else your business appears.

We provide your logo in multiple formats (SVG, PNG, PDF) and variations (full colour, reversed, monochrome) so you always have the right version for the right context.

### Colour palette
A considered set of brand colours with hex, RGB, and CMYK values — chosen to reflect your business personality and work consistently across digital and print.

### Typography
A defined set of typefaces for headings, body text, and supporting copy — creating a consistent typographic voice across all your communications.

### Brand guidelines
A concise brand guidelines document that captures your logo usage rules, colours, typography, and core visual style — so everyone who works on your brand uses it consistently.

### Supporting assets
We can also design supporting brand elements such as business cards, letterheads, social media profile images, and email signatures.

## Our process

1. **Brief** — We talk through your business, your audience, and what you want your brand to say about you.
2. **Concepts** — We develop initial design directions for you to review and respond to.
3. **Refinement** — We develop your chosen direction to a finished standard based on your feedback.
4. **Delivery** — You receive all final files, formats, and brand guidelines ready to use immediately.

## Built to grow with you

A well-designed brand scales. Whether you're building a website, running ads, printing leaflets, or pitching to clients, consistent branding makes everything look more professional and more trustworthy.
